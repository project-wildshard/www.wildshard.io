# ci is one job

- Meta TEST-SPEED TS16g: `e2e` folded into `check` in
  `.github/workflows/ci.yml`. Each job ran under a minute, so the second
  one only paid checkout, pnpm setup, the app token and the install a
  second time on another runner. Every step is kept, the Playwright
  Chromium install and the pnpm cache included; e2e runs last, after
  `site:build`, and the trace upload still fires on failure.
- The merged job moves to `ubuntu-24.04`: Playwright 1.60 can't install
  Chromium on ubuntu-latest (26.04), which is why e2e was pinned.
