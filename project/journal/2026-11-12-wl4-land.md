# WL4: land comes to www

www now lands plan worktrees through `land` (meta HOW-WE-BUILD WL4). ci runs on `plan/**`,
`land.config.json` names only `ci`, `/.worktrees/` is ignored, and `pnpm check` runs the kit's worktree guard
(red 4 of 8 before the ignore, 7 of 8 after with Vitest skipped: www tests with `node --test`).
www deploys a public site, so the deploy stays main-only: site-deploy.yml already runs on main pushes, and
its job now also skips on any other ref, so a `workflow_dispatch` on a plan branch can't ship it.
