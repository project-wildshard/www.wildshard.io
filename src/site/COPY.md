# What is Wildshard

## What · question

what is wildshard?

## What · title

One world, zone by zone.

## What · intro

An MMO in your browser, made of zones on one grid. Each zone is its own game, with its own look, quests and boss, built with Claude Code in its own repo and checked by the platform before it goes live. Today the Wildshard team builds them; next, creators do.

## What · points

- Walk one world of {=zonesWord} zones joined by roads, with other players in it.
- Fight, quest and ride, on your phone or at your desk.
- Every zone is built from one shard format, so anyone can add the next one.

# FAQ

## FAQ · question

questions

## FAQ · title

FAQ.

## FAQ · 1 question

What can I play today?

## FAQ · 1 answer

The MMO: {=zonesWord} zones on one grid, with other players, in any desktop or phone browser. Play as a guest with no account, or sign in with an email link or a passkey to keep your name. Every zone also has its own link to share.

## FAQ · 2 question

Is it free?

## FAQ · 2 answer

Playing is free, in your browser. Building is free too: the SDK is on npm, and you bring your own Claude Code. We never charge for uploading a zone.

## FAQ · 3 question

Which agent can build a zone?

## FAQ · 3 answer

We build every zone with Claude Code, and each zone's repo comes with its own Claude skills. The commands are plain `wildshard` commands, so any coding agent can drive them.

## FAQ · 4 question

Who owns my zone?

## FAQ · 4 answer

You do. You keep the rights to what you build; uploading lets us host it and show it in the world. Today every zone is the Wildshard team's, each in its own public repo.

## FAQ · 5 question

What can't a zone do?

## FAQ · 5 answer

Run JavaScript or shaders, reach your browser or another zone, go over its phone budgets, or block the roads into it. A shardfile holds data, models, images, sound and sandboxed WebAssembly, and the platform checks each one before it joins the grid.

## FAQ · 6 question

Can I upload a zone today?

## FAQ · 6 answer

Founding builders can: invited builders upload to the test world and get a link to share. Anyone can build and walk a zone on their own machine today, with `wildshard new` and `wildshard dev`. Uploads open wider after.

## MMO · shardfile question

what is a zone, exactly?

## MMO · shardfile title

The shardfile.

## MMO · shardfile intro

One sealed package: a `shard.json` manifest, and every other file named by its SHA-256. No functions and no custom shaders: data the engine reads, and sandboxed WebAssembly.

## MMO · shardfile note

The head of Mossgate Hollow's live shard.json, read from the grid: the template every new zone starts from

## MMO · shardfile sections label

What else is in a shard.json?

## MMO · shardfile sections

- `identity`: slug, name, author, save revision. Never a grid position: placement is the platform's.
- `requires` and `admission`: the engine it needs, and the caps and budgets it promises.
- `build`, `cook` and `preparation`: the source it came from, and what the server's cook made of it.
- `sections`: 35 hashed parts, each with its schema: the look from fixed families with a day cycle, a 60 Hz sim, state that is public, owner-only or host-only, four edge profiles, 62.5 m and 125 m tiles, creatures, encounters, quests, the ledger.
- `runtime: null`: no engine-side code at all.

## MMO · shardfile sections note

Format 0.1.0.6: {=schemaPaths} schema paths, and `wildshard schema` prints any of them. An unknown key fails.

Behaviour in a sandbox.

## MMO · sandbox intro

Yes, for new behaviour: a puzzle, a boss's mind, a gadget. It's written in AssemblyScript, compiled to WebAssembly, and run metered, with nothing to reach.

400K

## MMO · sandbox stat 3 label

fuel per call by default, 2M a decision tick

## MMO · sandbox stat 4

0

## MMO · sandbox stat 4 label

clocks, network, DOM or files; randomness only from seeded streams

## MMO · sandbox points

- **It only touches its own zone.** It writes its declared fields and sends commands within limits, and asks the world: walkable, path, ray, overlap, nearest, sight.
- **A runaway is stopped, not waited on.** Every loop pays fuel. On a trap nothing it wrote is kept, and after three failures the module is switched off.
- **The server owns the game.** A zone's sim scripts run on the server, about ten decisions a second; its view scripts run on the page.

Script ABI v1 · AssemblyScript first; other languages can target the same interface later

- **World chat, nearby chat and voice.** Voice is peer to peer, within a zone, up to 8 at once.
- **Two wallets, one ledger.** A zone's coins and bag stay in it; catalogue items and achievements travel with you, and a reward is granted once.

## MMO · together today

**Under the hood:** one world, run by four server workers, and each zone's `serverBudget` is read by the server.
